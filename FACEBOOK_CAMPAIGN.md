# PickUp AI — $50 Facebook Lead Test

## Goal

Generate qualified PEI business-owner inquiries for a free call-flow review and custom PickUp AI demo.

## Budget and schedule

- Total budget: CAD $50
- Duration: 5 days
- Daily budget: CAD $10
- Run continuously rather than dayparting; the budget is too small to fragment.
- Do not create multiple ad sets. Let one audience and one offer collect enough delivery to learn.

## Campaign setup

- Campaign objective: Leads
- Conversion location: Instant forms
- Buying type: Auction
- Campaign name: `PickUp AI | PEI | Free Call Review | $50 Test`
- Ad set name: `PEI Business Owners | Broad | 5 Days`
- Performance goal: Maximize number of leads
- Location: Prince Edward Island, Canada; people living in this location
- Age: 25–65+
- Language: English
- Audience: Start broad. If Ads Manager requires suggestions, use small business owners, entrepreneurship, restaurant owners, home improvement, tourism, and appointment scheduling as suggestions—not hard restrictions.
- Placements: Advantage+ placements
- Schedule: Five full days beginning after the form, privacy link, and notification email have been tested.

## Tracking

Use this landing-page URL anywhere the ad links to the site:

`https://listedpei.ca/pickupai?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=pickupai_pei_launch&utm_content=missed_calls_v1`

Track daily:

- Amount spent
- Reach and frequency
- Link click-through rate
- Instant form opens
- Instant form completions
- Cost per lead
- Qualified leads
- Sales conversations booked

Primary decision metric: qualified leads, not clicks.

## Stop/continue rule

- Do not make changes during the first CAD $20 unless the ad is rejected or broken.
- At CAD $30, verify that the form opens and notifications work.
- At CAD $50, continue only if at least one genuinely relevant PEI business lead was generated or the campaign produced a strong form-open rate that supports a revised offer.
- Do not increase the budget based only on reach, reactions, or cheap clicks.

## Lead follow-up

Respond as quickly as practical during business hours. Use this opening:

> Hi {{first_name}}, thanks for requesting a PickUp AI call-flow review for {{business_name}}. I saw that your biggest phone challenge is {{challenge}}. I have a couple of ideas for how the agent could handle those calls. Would a quick 15-minute conversation on {{option_1}} or {{option_2}} work?

The first follow-up should reference the prospect's selected challenge and business instead of repeating a generic product pitch.
